# Team Kit

A starter kit for running a small business with a team of Claude Code sessions. Each session has a role: a manager that talks to you, writers and shop helpers that do the work, a reviewer that checks it, and a publisher that puts it live. A flight board on your phone shows what is in progress and what needs your decision.

**Who it is for:** a business owner who is not a developer and wants Claude to do the day-to-day work, with checks in place.

**How to start:** open [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md). It takes about 15 minutes, with exact clicks.

**What you get:**
- `CLAUDE.md`: the way the team works, written for any business.
- `docs/TEAM.md`: who does what, and the rules for asking you questions.
- `packs/`: role briefs for writing, shop, sales, operations and software work.
- `/team-init`: asks you ten questions, then writes your business's own files.
- The flight board: a page you can open on a phone.

**Built from how a working team of Claude Code sessions is run in practice.** [LESSONS.md](LESSONS.md) lists the traps that shaped these rules.
